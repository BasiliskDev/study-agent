      Insertion cheat sheet
      1. BST-insert the new node z and color it red. NIL leaves count as black.
      2. While z's parent p is red, look at the uncle u (p's sibling) and grandparent g:
        1. Case 1 u is red: color p and u black, g red. Set z = g and repeat.
        2. Case 2 u is black, z is an inner child (triangle): rotate at p, away from z (z is the right child of a left p, so rotate left). The old p becomes the new z.
        3. Case 3 u is black, z is an outer child (straight line): color p black, g red, and rotate at g the other way (left-left means rotate right). Done.
      3. Finally, color the root black.

      Doing a rotation by hand

      Rotate left at x, where y is x's right child and b is y's left child:

      1. Cut the edge above x, the edge x–y, and the edge y–b.
      2. Connect y where x used to hang (or leave it as the root).
      3. Connect x as y's left child, then b as x's right child.

      Rotate right is the mirror image. Keys: C recolors the selected node, Delete cuts it from its parent, Z/Y undo/redo, F fits the view.
